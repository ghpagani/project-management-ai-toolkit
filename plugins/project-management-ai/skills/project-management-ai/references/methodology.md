# Methodology / Metodologia

CPM: forward pass ES=max(predecessor EF), EF=ES+duration; backward pass LF=min(successor LS), LS=LF-duration. Terminal LF is total project duration. Float=LS−ES. Zero float is critical within the model. Multiple critical paths are possible; no single path is asserted.

Earned value: CV=EV−AC; SV=EV−PV (currency, not days); CPI=EV/AC; SPI=EV/PV; EAC=BAC×AC/EV assuming current cost efficiency continues; ETC=EAC−AC; VAC=BAC−EAC. No EAC when EV or AC is zero. Assumptions require human verification. Report ratios are rounded only after calculation. See GAO Cost and Schedule guides in sources.

Risk score = ordinal probability × ordinal impact; threshold is supplied by the user, not a universal rule. RACI checks count responsible and accountable roles, not authority or individual capacity.

Planning support (guidance only, not executable validators): initiation: state problem, expected benefit, sponsor, constraints and decision rights. Planning: define acceptance criteria, deliverables, dependencies and baseline; identify stakeholders and risks. Delivery: choose predictive, iterative or hybrid approach according to uncertainty, constraints and feedback; review work in progress, changes and blockers. Closure: review acceptance, handover, remaining risks and lessons. For agile work do not impose fixed velocity as a target or equate story points across teams. For development projects include participation, safeguarding, sustainability and accountability. These are original prompts, not reproductions of a standard.
